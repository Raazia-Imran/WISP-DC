# Source inclusion notes

The repository contains the research code, inputs, outputs, final combined workbook, original source workbooks and notes, prototype catalogue, rule tables, formula workbooks, current working manuscript, and the teacher-supplied Elsevier template.

The internal module planning document was excluded because it contains private editing-capability links and superseded project-planning text. No private edit links are included here. Publisher research PDFs in the Literature folder are not mirrored; the paper bibliography, reference workbook and literature matrix identify the sources instead. Grammar-review files and Word conversion internals are not research evidence and are not published.

Archived notes and workbooks preserve the earlier research history, including provisional cloud prices. They are not the authority for the paper-profile conclusions. The broader prototype/rules and prototype/formula_workbooks directories are supporting design material, not additional validated S1/S2 performance evidence.
